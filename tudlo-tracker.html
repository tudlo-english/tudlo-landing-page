What's inside

One simple habit. Everything else follows.

Add a student, schedule their lessons, and mark each class after it happens. The money screens count those records for you, so the numbers always agree.

01

Every student, at a glance.

Keep each learner's details together: where they live, what time it is for them right now, their rate, book, lesson and attendance.

  • Their local time shown next to yours
  • Current book, lesson and page from the latest class
  • Contract progress and attendance on the same page
02

Your week, on your clock.

Add weekly or one-off classes. Time zones are handled for you: a class is saved on the student's clock and shown on yours.

  • Weekly calendar with colour-coded class statuses
  • Move one class without touching the weekly routine
  • Close the times you are not available
03

Mark classes in seconds.

After a lesson, record what happened. Forgot a few? Tick them in the Needs status list and mark them all in one go.

  • Present, Absent, Cancelled, Rescheduled, No Show and more
  • Choose which statuses count toward the bill
  • A bell reminds you of anything left unmarked
04

Always know who has paid.

Bill monthly, by contract or per class. Tudlo Tracker counts your marked classes and shows what is due, paid and outstanding.

  • Monthly, Contract and Per-class students, side by side
  • Every currency kept separate, never mixed together
  • Record a payment in a few clicks
05

Send a statement in one click.

Make a printable PDF statement for any student, with their classes, payments and balance. Export everything to Excel or CSV whenever you need it.

  • Classes listed in the student's own time zone
  • Monthly billing, attendance and class totals in Reports
  • One Excel workbook with a sheet for each part of your records

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Email support.tudlo@gmail.com.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Tudlo Tracker runs on your computer and works with no connection at all. Nothing about your students is sent to the internet.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer only. The app backs itself up once a day and keeps the last 30 backups. You can also make one yourself, download it, and copy it to a USB stick or Google Drive for safekeeping.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows PCs, and Macs with either an Apple chip (M1 and newer) or an Intel processor. We'll send the installer that matches your computer.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Each license is made for one computer. If you teach from a laptop and a desktop, message us and we'll sort out a license for each.

What happens when my license ends?

The app keeps working for 7 more days. After that it becomes view-only: you can still look at everything, make backups and export, but you can't add or change anything until you renew. Nothing is ever deleted. To renew, we send you a new license file. You don't reinstall and your data doesn't change.

What if I get a new computer?

Install the app on the new computer and send us its code. We'll make a new license with the same end date, and you restore your latest backup. Everything comes back as it was.
